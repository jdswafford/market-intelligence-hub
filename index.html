Site Transparency, Executive Methodology & Data Provenance

1. Live News Syndication, 90-Day Recency & Imagery Engine

All dynamic news signals are queried in real time via Google News RSS (`news.google.com/rss/search`) paired with a strict 90-day recency window (`when:3m`) and verified against the `MAX_NEWS_AGE_DAYS = 90` timestamp gate in JavaScript.

Source Governance & Live Imagery: Per internal governance rules, `arkance.world` is strictly excluded via `-site:arkance.world` query parameters and client-side URL sanitizers, routing ARKANCE coverage to verified `arkance.us` and tier-1 trade publications. Card imagery is dynamically extracted from RSS media enclosures and live OpenGraph (`og:image`) metadata via Microlink API with verified fallback protection.

2. Macroeconomic, Supply Chain & Labor Provenance

Macroeconomic and operational indicators are sourced directly from institutional releases: the AIA Architecture Billings Index (ABI), Dodge Momentum Index (DMI), ABC Construction Backlog Indicator (CBI), Federal Reserve SLOOS Report, and U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Producer Price Index (PPI) & Employment Situation reports.

Private equity roll-up and AEC M&A consolidation velocity is cross-referenced against quarterly deal trackers from Morrissey Goodale and FMI Capital Advisors.

3. OEM vs. Channel vs. Advisory Taxonomy

To prevent conflating software creators with channel partners, the hub strictly separates: (1) Industry Software Matrix (12 Software OEMs including Procore, Autodesk, Bentley, Trimble, Bluebeam/Nemetschek, Egnyte, Topcon, Aurigo, HCSS, Slate.ai, OFC Desk, CivCore), (2) Channel Matrix (6 Global VARs: ARKANCE, Graitec, Symetri, NTI Group, IMAGINiT, Man and Machine), and (3) Advisory Firm Matrix (7 Global Consultancies: Capgemini, Deloitte, Accenture, McKinsey, PwC, EY, KPMG).

4. Regulatory, Delivery & Vertical Classification

Every entity and news item is dual-tagged by Regional Market (`AMER`, `EMEA`, `APAC`) and Vertical Sector (`Data Centers & Mfg`, `Energy & Grid`, `Civil & Transport`, `Healthcare & Life Sci`, `Commercial & Inst`).
