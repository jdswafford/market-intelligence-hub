Scoring Key & Evaluation Methodology Guide

1. Cloud Maturity (Scale 1–10) Measures the depth of a competitor's cloud-native architecture, Common Data Environment (CDE) scalability, SaaS subscription transition velocity, and enterprise cloud hosting readiness.
2. Channel Threat (Scale 1–10) Evaluates direct head-to-head overlap in target enterprise accounts, aggressive pricing or bundling displacement, and territorial competition across North America, EMEA, and ANZ.
3. Interoperability (Scale 1–10) Assesses open BIM standard compliance (IFC/ISO 19650), bidirectional API connectors with Autodesk/Nemetschek/ERP ecosystems, and multi-platform workflow integration.
4. M&A Velocity (Scale 1–10) Tracks the pace and capital intensity of acquiring regional value-added resellers (VARs), proprietary software plug-in developers, and specialized AEC consulting practices.
5. Composite Threat Score (Weighted Average) Synthesizes all four dimensions into a single executive risk index. 8.0–10.0 (Critical Tier): Requires active executive counter-strategy and account defense. 7.0–7.9 (High Watch): Requires regional sales battlecards and solution differentiation.
