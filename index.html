Scoring Key & Evaluation Methodology Guide

1. Cloud Maturity

Measures transition velocity from legacy desktop seats to cloud-native Common Data Environments (CDEs), multi-tenant SaaS architectures, and token/consumption telemetry.

2. Channel Threat

Evaluates direct pressure on partner margins, including publisher direct-to-enterprise billing, aggressive service discounting, and rival proprietary IP bundling.

3. Interoperability

Assesses API openness, openBIM/IFC compliance, and cross-platform data connectors linking design models to ERP, GIS, and jobsite hardware telemetry.

4. M&A Velocity

Tracks the frequency and scale of strategic acquisitions, private equity roll-ups, and regional VAR consolidation expanding the competitor's footprint.

5. Composite Threat Score

Weighted executive index across all dimensions: 8.5–10.0 (Critical Priority), 8.0–8.4 (High Watch), and 7.0–7.9 (Moderate Rival).
